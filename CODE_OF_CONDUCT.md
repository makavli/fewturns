# Code of Conduct

This project is a small, shared piece of work. Everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull requests and discussions, is expected to make it a place where others want to take part too.

## What we ask

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most disagreements here are about a setting, not about a person.
- Criticise the work, with specifics and a way forward.
- Accept that the maintainers decide what goes in, and that "no" to a change is not a judgement of its author.
- Keep other people's private details private, including what a pasted audit or log may reveal.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to the maintainers through the repository's [security advisory form](https://github.com/makavli/fewturns/security/advisories/new), which only they can read. They will look into every report, keep the reporter's identity to themselves, and may remove comments, close threads or block an account that keeps breaking these rules.
